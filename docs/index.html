Windows, macOS & Linux. Free core, local-first, no subscription required.

  • Open the file you were sent

    Import Word, RTF, PDF, plain text and Markdown and get clean prompter text — paragraphs kept, formatting flattened, typographic quotes and dashes normalised. It tells you what it could not bring across, and saves nothing until you say so.

  • Find your way around a long script

    Start a line with # and it becomes a section marker: a jump list beside the seek bar, ticks on the bar itself, and next/previous to walk them. Plus find & replace and live word, character and long-line counts.

  • A real reading pace

    Set speed in characters per second or by BPM (20–250) to read lyrics and verses in time with music — not an arbitrary 1–10 slider.

  • Pause chips (caesuras)

    Type -- for a beat or --N for an N-second hold; each becomes an atomic marker, lit one at a time as you read.

  • Preview = projector

    One character-based engine keeps the operator preview, the talent projector, and any LAN device in step — even at different fonts.

  • Autocomplete, offline

    Ghost-text word and phrase suggestions from bundled per-language dictionaries — 18 languages, instant, and entirely on your machine. Tab accepts, Esc dismisses.

  • Write your script by speaking

    Press record and talk — what you say is written straight into the script. Nothing to train, nothing to set up. Recognition runs on your machine with a speech model that ships inside the app, so it works with the network cable pulled out, and the mic opens only while you're recording.

  • Read to time, not to a guess

    Tap your own tempo and the BPM is calibrated to your delivery, not an assumed one. Optional click with a count-in, a live bar and beat counter, and bar lines on the seek bar.

  • Rehearse, and find out where you ran long

    Read it through once and get a section-by-section report — planned against actual, and the pace you really delivered it at. It records timings only: no microphone, no audio, ever.

  • Labels that cost no time

    Mark Chorus, [Verse 1] or ## Bridge as words you don't perform. You still see them coming, but they take zero read time — so your lyrics stay on the bar you wrote them for.

  • Start countdown & read-aloud

    An optional 3-2-1 pre-roll on preview and projector, plus a read-aloud preview through your operating system's own speech engine.

  • Yours, privately

    Local-first and account-free — your scripts stay on your machine. No AI, no telemetry, nothing sent anywhere. Permissively licensed end to end.
